# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in CapsAwake a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include respect, constructive feedback, accountability, and focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, publishing private information, or other conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through the security or contact channel listed in the repository.
